Schedule & Topics

Two hands-on mornings. Each stands on its own: come to either day, or take both for more practice.

The times illustrate an 8:00 a.m.–noon format. Each work block allows about 8 minutes for an idea, 30 minutes to try it, 7 minutes to discuss what happened, and 5 minutes to set up the next step.

You can use your own permitted materials or our fictional Cedarline practice folders. The two folders are separate; get the materials before class if you like.

A course habit

Ask freely. Approve actions carefully.

Ask the tool what it can see, what it needs, and what it proposes. Review consequential changes before allowing them.

Session one · working plan

Breaking Free from Chat

8:00 a.m.–noon

8:00–8:50

Block 01 · Getting oriented

Set up your workspace

Learn what it means to work beyond chat in ChatGPT Work or Claude Cowork: choose a project, a model, and a bounded working folder; review privacy and permission settings; confirm what the tool can access.

Why it matters: Model choice affects cost, and the working environment affects what the tool can do.

8:50–9:00Break · 10 minutes

9:00–9:50

Block 02 · File work

Work with files

See how the tool can inspect files, name them by their contents, and propose a useful folder structure. Work on a copy: ask for a plan, approve selected changes, then account for every original file.

Also: Decide which work to delegate and which changes require your judgment.

9:50–10:00Break · 10 minutes

10:00–10:50

Block 03 · Understanding your work

Context is key

Give the tool relevant files, your goal and audience, constraints, preferences, and an appropriate writing sample. Ask what else it needs and what it has learned from the folder; keep the context useful and the access bounded.

Block 04 · Making something useful

Create an output

See what forms the work could take—Markdown, a Word document, PDF, HTML, a shared document, or a small website. Choose one useful output, then check its facts, fit, and voice against the source material.

Also: Ask well, verify hard, and keep the final judgment yours.

Session two · working plan

From Feedback to Decision

8:00 a.m.–noon

A fresh case begins here. You will turn feedback into a recommendation and test a small aid on new information. The exact pacing may change after our dry run.

8:00–8:50

Block 05 · Frame and research

Ask a question worth checking

Start with Dana's decision and the Day 2 case background. Learn to check an AI claim against its original source and decide whether it really applies to this situation.

8:50–9:00Break · 10 minutes

9:00–9:50

Block 06 · Evidence

Find a defensible result

Read the survey, count a theme, and keep the response IDs behind your number. Separate survey patterns from support requests; note what the records cannot tell you.

9:50–10:00Break · 10 minutes

10:00–10:50

Block 07 · Build and write

Make something Dana can use

Make a small working table, spreadsheet, or local page that shows checked theme counts and their source IDs. Write a short recommendation in your own voice and test the aid against the original records.

10:50–11:00Break · 10 minutes

11:00–11:50

Block 08 · Reuse and boundaries

Try it on fresh feedback

Run your method on a separate new batch. See what breaks, revise one step, and set personal rules for what AI may access, what you verify, and what you approve.
