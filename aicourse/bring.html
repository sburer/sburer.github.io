Preparing Your Materials

Come with one work task or personal goal you would genuinely like to advance. It does not need to be polished or important-looking. It just needs to matter to you.

WORK

A slow, repetitive task

A report, recurring update, spreadsheet, research question, stack of documents, or planning task.

LIFE

A personal goal

A purchase decision, trip, family project, learning plan, or other goal from your own life.

MATERIAL

Permitted examples

Bring only files you are authorized to use. Public, personal, sanitized, and fictional examples are all welcome.
