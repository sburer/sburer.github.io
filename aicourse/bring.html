Prefer your own task?

Bring a slow, repetitive work task or a personal goal that matters to you. A report, research question, small set of documents, spreadsheet, comparison, or plan can all work. Bring only information you are authorized to use; remove private details when possible. Keep a safe copy and choose a task small enough to make progress during class.

Corporate accounts and devices may limit file access or AI features. The supplied packets give you a fallback without needing real workplace data.
